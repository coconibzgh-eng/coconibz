GOODNESS FROM GHANA

Why Coconibz?

Pure Coconut. Pure Ghana. Pure Goodness.

Get to know the ingredients and naturally occurring nutrients in our three coconut drinks. Every flavor brings its own taste and goodness.

Explore our flavors ↗

01 / THE PURE ORIGINAL

Coconut Classic

Light, refreshing coconut goodness in its simplest form.

Nutrients associated with coconut

  • Healthy fats: medium-chain triglycerides (MCTs), including lauric acid
  • Minerals: potassium, magnesium, manganese, iron, phosphorus, zinc, and selenium
  • Vitamins: vitamin C, vitamin E, and B vitamins including B1, B3, B6, and folate
  • Natural electrolytes
  • Small amounts of dietary fiber and plant protein

Ways it may fit into a balanced lifestyle

  • Coconut fats can be used by the body as an energy source; individual responses vary.
  • A refreshing drink choice that can contribute fluids to your day.
  • Coconut contains lauric acid and other fats; this does not mean the drink prevents or treats infections.
  • Its fat content may help some people feel satisfied between meals.
  • A plant-based alternative to dairy when the product's ingredient label confirms it is dairy-free.

A flavor to consider: Anyone who enjoys a simple coconut taste and a refreshing drink. Check the ingredient label for your dietary needs.

02 / NATURALLY SWEETENED

Coconut with Dates

Coconut blended with the rich, naturally sweet taste of dates.

Nutrients associated with dates

Alongside the nutrients associated with coconut, dates can contribute:

  • Natural sugars: glucose and fructose
  • Fiber: soluble and insoluble fiber
  • Minerals: potassium, iron, calcium, and copper
  • Vitamins: including B6, K, and A
  • Plant compounds: flavonoids, carotenoids, and phenolic acids
  • Natural amino acids

Ways it may fit into a balanced lifestyle

  • Dates provide naturally occurring sugars and can make this flavor sweeter than Classic.
  • Dates contain fiber, which contributes to overall dietary fiber intake.
  • Dates provide minerals and plant compounds as part of a varied diet.
  • A naturally sweet-tasting option for people who enjoy fruit-based sweetness.
  • Because dates contain natural sugars, people managing blood sugar should check the nutrition information and ask a healthcare professional for personal advice.

A flavor to consider: People who prefer a naturally sweet taste. Dates still contribute sugars, so check the product's nutrition information if monitoring sugar intake.

03 / EXTRA MILKY & CREAMY

Coconut with Tigernut

A creamy blend with the distinctive, gently sweet taste of tigernut.

Nutrients associated with tigernut

Alongside the nutrients associated with coconut, tigernut can contribute:

  • Fiber: including resistant starch
  • Vitamins: vitamin E and vitamin C
  • Minerals: iron, magnesium, phosphorus, zinc, and potassium
  • Healthy fats: including oleic acid
  • Plant protein and the amino acid arginine
  • Natural plant compounds and enzymes

Ways it may fit into a balanced lifestyle

  • Its creamy character can make it enjoyable in smoothies or with cereal; check the flavor and product label for your intended use.
  • Tigernut fiber, including resistant starch, contributes to fiber intake.
  • Tigernut contains oleic acid and arginine; the presence of these nutrients does not guarantee changes to blood pressure or circulation.
  • A non-dairy option only when the product's actual ingredients and handling meet your needs.
  • Contains naturally occurring ingredients; it is not a treatment for digestive, reproductive, blood-sugar, or other health conditions.

A flavor to consider: People who enjoy a richer, creamier taste. Review the ingredients and allergen information before choosing.

COCONIBZ is a food beverage, not medicine.
