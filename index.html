OUR LITTLE STORY

About Coconibz.

Pure Coconut. Pure Ghana. Pure Goodness.

Welcome to Coconibz, where the natural power of coconut meets authentic Ghanaian goodness.

Coconibz is a premium, all-natural coconut beverage proudly produced by NAKEL'S FOODIE, a 100% Ghanaian-owned company registered and recognized by the GRA. Born in the heart of Tamale, Asawaba, we are on a mission to redefine healthy refreshment in Ghana, one bottle at a time.

We believe what you drink should nourish you. That's why Coconibz is made with absolutely no added sugar, no additives, and no preservatives. Just fresh coconut, treated drinking water, and nature's own sweeteners.

Discover Your Favorite Flavor

  1. Coconut Classic — The pure, unfiltered taste of fresh coconut milk. Smooth, refreshing, and original.
  2. Coconut with Dates — Naturally sweetened with premium dates. A healthy sweetness your body will thank you for.
  3. Coconut with Tigernut — Our extra-creamy blend. Enriched with tigernut for that extra milky richness and energy boost.

Every bottle is 100% natural, organic, and made under strict hygienic conditions.

We are proud to be a product recognized and supported by the Jim Leech Foundation of Queen's University and the Mastercard Foundation, a testament to our quality, innovation, and commitment to youth-led Ghanaian enterprise.

From our kitchen in Tamale to events across the country, Coconibz has refreshed guests at weddings, corporate events, and private parties. And with our nationwide delivery, your favorite healthy drink is never too far away.
